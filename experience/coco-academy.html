Product Director - Coco Academy

Role Overview

As Product Director at Coco Academy, a hands-on STEAM makerspace for K-12 students in South Philadelphia, I lead all engineering programming across 2 locations serving 700+ students annually. I oversee curriculum development, instructor hiring and training, and day-to-day operations for a team of 15, including 4 full-time staff. Programs consistently sell out within 24 hours of opening and have delivered 5,200+ student project builds across 80+ unique project types. In 2025, Coco Academy was recognized as a Philadelphia Family LOVE Award winner for STEAM Enrichment Programming.
